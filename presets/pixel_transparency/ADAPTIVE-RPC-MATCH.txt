Source-sized RPC Sunlight Shimmer with matched presentation
=========================================================

sunlight_shimmer-adaptive-rpc-match-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp

This separate profile keeps Adaptive RPC's cached shadows and sunlight,
full paper texture, seven rainbow colors, full-precision shadow buffers and
256x256 optical field. It renders the final compositor to a viewport-sized
target before RetroArch's stock presentation copy, giving eight Vulkan passes
instead of seven. It also emulates the original transparency pass's observed
FP16 truncation before applying sunlight, within the combined compositor.
The LCD stages, parameter defaults and existing presets are unchanged.

Why matching needs these two changes
-----------------------------------

The original pipeline composites into an offscreen target. Rendering directly
to a letterboxed viewport can produce slightly different interpolated texture
coordinates. Near a native pixel boundary, this can choose a different source
pixel; the procedural paper hash can also change. Matching the original target
removes the large differences observed in the macOS comparison.

The original transparency pass writes an FP16 target before sunlight. Combining
the passes skips that storage conversion and produces a small brightness shift.
The matching compositor reproduces the observed truncation with packed-half
conversions, without restoring another full-resolution transparency pass.
Shadow kernels and spectral calculations remain in the small cache passes.

Image checks (2026-10-15)
-------------------------

Paired macOS RetroArch Vulkan captures use the same native saved-frame images:
Mario Tennis slot 9 (160x144) and Pokemon Lazarus slot 0 (240x160), supplied by
RetroArch's built-in imageviewer. Each capture ends at frame 10; motion is off
in both presets. Screen fit uses 1200x1080 and 1240x827 viewports respectively;
integer scaling uses 1120x1008 and 1200x800.

All eight default/Fast-shadow comparisons differ from the original by at most
one 8-bit channel level. This bound also holds at screen fit with shadows or
sunlight disabled, maximum blur/offset, zero offset, Simple brightness,
reflection enabled, zero backing brightness and complete bypass: twenty-four
comparisons in total. Bypass matches exactly. With sunlight disabled, only one
pixel differs by one level in each game. Default average luminance differs by
less than 0.01 percent across the four game/scale combinations.

The previous direct-output profile reached 85 levels at GBC screen fit and 23
at GBA screen fit in these Mac captures. The LCD-only outputs already matched.

This is not a bit-exact reconstruction of the original sunlight calculations.
The 256x256 optical-field approximation remains. Combined extreme optical
settings reach eleven levels on the GBC frame and three on the GBA frame.
The existing shadow-cache rounding differences on larger inputs also remain.

Keep the paired caches and compositor together. This profile does not restore
the original RPC profile's 160x144 source restriction. The coordinate and FP16
rounding match is validated on macOS Vulkan. Physical tilt, matching on the RPC,
and RPC performance of this variant have not been tested; the device was
disconnected during these checks.

Local timing check (2026-10-15)
------------------------------

Three alternating runs per profile render 1,800 static Mario Tennis frames
at 1200x1080, with audio, audio sync and vsync disabled. Median whole-process
time is 15.675 seconds for Adaptive RPC and 15.680 seconds for this profile:
no meaningful difference at this measurement's resolution. Timings include
startup, screenshot and shutdown; these are not isolated GPU pass times.
They are consistent with a 120 Hz presentation limit and cannot establish
GPU headroom or predict the Retroid Pocket Classic's frame rate.

Vertex and fragment shader builds pass SPIR-V validation with sensor uniforms
both enabled and disabled. The 61 parameter definitions, their order, labels,
defaults and ranges match the existing presets.
