Timing method
-------------
Gambatte, copied Mario Tennis slot 9; native 160 x 144. Same frame window
128-384 after state load. Each result is the median of run medians; each
run uses at least two OCR readings of RetroArch's FPS counter. Final runs
are interleaved current/below/above, with reverse ordering in the second
round where present. See benchmark-summary.json for run/sample counts and
ranges, benchmark-runs.json for exact frames, logs, battery temperatures,
and intermediate pilots. Superseded pilot artifact paths are marked. Only final-labelled runs appear
in the table. Vsync, audio synchronization, frameskip and runahead disabled.
These are short title-screen throughput checks, not a sustained thermal
soak or guarantees for other games, resolutions, settings or hardware.

Above intentionally changes placement and brightness. It preserves the
full-resolution effects field rather than literally prepending at native
resolution. Do not interpret its below/above differences as regressions.
Authentic uses the existing verified axis-cache implementation, including
its optional subpixel geometry and line-colour controls.

Source checks: every new vertex/fragment stage compiled and passed SPIR-V
validation with sensor uniforms both enabled and disabled. All 12 presets
resolved their dependencies and preserved inherited parameter definitions
and overrides. Device logs were checked for the expected shader names and
rejected compilation, reflection, semantic and fallback errors.

A combined single-pass prototype for the four simple grids was slower on
RPC and was discarded. A separate native-colour cache for LCD Grid v2 also
failed to improve its throughput and was removed. The retained shared implementation keeps the input
pass separate. Geometry caches improve LCD Grid v2, but the
live toggle does not guarantee no FPS loss for every family. In particular,
Authentic above remains below the 60-FPS headroom target on this workload.
