Retired Pixel Transparency experiments
=====================================

These are historical records, not runnable preset folders. On 2026-10-18,
the following routes were removed from presets/pixel_transparency_experiments/
and handheld/shaders/pixel_transparency_experiments/:

  v2.4-append               Slower all-append route: 15 presets.
  v2.4-fused-authentic      Fused Authentic prototype: 2 presets.
  v2.4-integrated-authentic Full-screen grid-cache prototype: 2 presets.

The nine original notes are preserved byte-for-byte below. Instructions and
relative paths inside them describe the former layout; they are historical.
SHADERS.txt is the former v2.4-append shader-folder README.txt. All other notes
came from the matching preset folder. The retired code remains in local
version-control history. External captures, logs and comparison pages were
not moved or deleted. Installed device bundles were not changed.

Use ../v2.4-integrated-grids/ for dedicated styles with live placement, or
../v2.4-live-grid/ for live style selection too. The retained Authentic axis
caches now own the shared grid controls previously in the retired integrated
folder. Production fast presets and fixed before/after variants remain intact.
