Retroid Pocket Classic profiles
==============================

pixel_transparency-rpc-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp
sunlight_shimmer-rpc-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp

Use these with native 160x144 GB/GBC input and keep the compositor last.
Larger or software-upscaled input bypasses the added effects. Use the
existing general presets for GBA, larger sources, or additional post-passes.
The original presets and the Authentic GBC shader files are unchanged.

Both profiles preserve the original parameter labels, ordering, defaults,
ranges, and preset overrides. Full paper texture remains the default;
Sunlight Shimmer retains all seven spectral colors.

Pipeline
--------

The native linearization and full-resolution LCD passes remain separate.
A small full-precision cache stores the nearest-filtered shadow kernel's
three horizontal and three vertical sampling phases, including the screen
edges and larger blur settings. The final pass reads the appropriate value.

Shimmer also computes spectral tint and glare in a 256x256 RGBA16F field.
The game image, LCD grid, compositing, and front-surface reflection remain
at output resolution. Light-field coordinates and uniform palette values
are calculated in the vertex stage. The final pass renders directly to the
viewport and applies dither in viewport-local coordinates.

The Vulkan pipelines execute four passes for transparency and six for
shimmer. These counts include the hidden copy pass in the original presets:
the new profiles replace expensive viewport work with smaller cache passes.

Precision and scope
-------------------

The optical field is interpolated. At default settings, the tested shimmer
captures differed from the original by at most one 8-bit channel level.
Extreme film scale/noise, color shift, and close-light settings can differ
more; the combined stress case reached twelve levels. Use the original
Sunlight Shimmer preset when full-resolution optics are more important than
throughput at such settings.

The shadow cache assumes nearest original-image sampling, as configured by
these presets. Its padded domain covers the supplied blur range at normal
RPC viewport ratios. The cache and compositor must be kept together.

Validation
----------

Retroid Pocket Classic, Adreno A12, RetroArch Vulkan, Gambatte, Mario Tennis,
copied save slot 9. Uncapped comparisons sample the same title-screen frame
window (128 through 320), with audio, vsync, frame skipping, and runahead off.
Screen fit is 1200x1080; integer scaling is 1120x1008.

Measured on 2026-10-15, with default quality settings:

                              Screen fit        Integer
Pixel Transparency             77.7 FPS         88.4 FPS
Sunlight Shimmer               61.1 FPS         69.7 FPS

Screen-fit results are medians of three run medians. Integer results are
single matched-frame runs. Normal-speed playback was approximately 60 FPS;
a longer shimmer observation ranged from 59.65 to 60.25 FPS. Shimmer has
limited uncapped headroom at screen fit; integer scaling offers more margin.

Final source builds passed vertex/fragment compilation and SPIR-V validation
with sensor uniforms enabled and disabled. Parameter lists match the general
presets. Device captures cover both scale modes, shadow edges and blur,
brightness modes, transparency modes, zero contributions, bypass, reflection,
debug output, and stronger optical settings.

Performance numbers are whole-pipeline throughput for the tested scene,
not isolated GPU pass times or a guarantee for every game and setting.
Normal Game Boy Color playback targets approximately 59.73 Hz.
