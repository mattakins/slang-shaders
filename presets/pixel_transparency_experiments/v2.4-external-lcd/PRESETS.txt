Pixel Transparency v2.4 — external LCD pipeline
===============================================

Production until 2026-10-21, kept as an alternative. The integrated grid
presets in ../../pixel_transparency/ replaced it. This pipeline runs the
original LCD shader as its own pass, so it can also be appended to any LCD
shader: use pixel_transparency-append.slangp or sunlight_shimmer-append.slangp
here. authentic-gbc-frontlit.slangp is its former frontlit preset.

The standard presets use the Adaptive RPC shadow-cache pipeline. Sunlight
Shimmer retains its 256x256 optical field, full paper texture, seven rainbow
colors and final anti-banding dither. LCD rendering remains a separate stage.
Parameter defaults and ranges are preserved; the version heading is v2.4.
Paired presets share one shadow_cache.slang entry point; transparency controls
now appear before LCD controls. This release does not enable the optional Match
presentation path.

Recommended Authentic GBC Fast entries:

  pixel_transparency-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp   5 passes
  sunlight_shimmer-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp     7 passes

The lcd1x, lcd3x, lcd-grid-v2, simpletex_lcd and zfast_lcd variants use the same
v2.4 program with their original LCD shaders and settings. Plain transparency
uses 4 passes and Sunlight Shimmer 6 passes with these single-stage LCDs.

Fixed grid placement variants
-----------------------------

The grid-before/ and grid-after/ subfolders each contain ten presets: plain
Pixel Transparency and Sunlight Shimmer for lcd1x, lcd3x, lcd-grid-v2,
simpletex_lcd and zfast_lcd. "Before/after" describes the grid's position
relative to the transparency and sunlight effects.

  grid-before/   Grid below the effects; references the unchanged standard
                 presets. 4 passes for transparency, 6 for Shimmer.
  grid-after/    Grid above the effects, retaining full-resolution paper and
                 light detail. 5 passes for transparency, 7 for Shimmer.

Choose the matching filename inside either folder. Placement is selected by
preset, not by a live toggle. Existing filenames and defaults remain intact.
Authentic GBC Fast and the other five families have live-placement presets
in production: ../../pixel_transparency/
For live grid-style selection too, use: ../v2.4-live-grid/

Grid-after keeps the native shadow cache and adds final display dither after
LCD rendering. Two viewport-sized 16-bit float intermediates preserve colour
precision until that final pass. These variants require more framebuffer
bandwidth than the standard presets; RPC performance has not been measured.
They are alternate presentations, not replacements for the fast defaults.

Four families use the original LCD shaders. LCD Grid v2 uses an adapter with
its existing grid geometry/settings but samples effects at output resolution
instead of four native pixel centres. That avoids downsampling paper and light
detail. It is deliberately different from literally prepending the effects to
unmodified LCD Grid v2. LCD gamma, darkening, colour and background controls
now act on the completed effects image as well as the game in grid-after.
Dithering remains last; the Shimmer optical field stays 256x256 and uses the
displayed aspect ratio, including stretched content.

Standalone / append workflow
----------------------------

To use another grid/LCD preset, load it first, then choose Append Preset and
select handheld/pixel_transparency.slangp or handheld/sunlight_shimmer.slangp.
Append the whole preset, not an individual .slang file. No LCD-specific shadow
cache wrapper is needed: both append presets use the generic shadow_cache.slang
and preserve the grid's rendered image before preparing the cache. Their bounded
cache covers inputs through 511x511;
larger inputs use the complete shadow kernel instead of bypassing the effects.
These standalone sequences use 3 and 5 passes respectively, in addition to the
LCD preset. The paired presets above allocate their cache at native input size
and are the recommended Retroid route. Standalone throughput is not benchmarked.

Previous versions
-----------------

All former v2.3 programs and presets, including Original, RPC, Adaptive,
Adaptive Fast, Adaptive RPC and Match, are archived under:

  presets/pixel_transparency_experiments/v2.3/
  handheld/shaders/pixel_transparency_experiments/v2.3/

This folder retains the 12 standard v2.4 presets at its top level. Historical RPC,
Adaptive, Adaptive Fast, Adaptive RPC and Match entries are available directly
in presets/pixel_transparency_experiments/v2.3/. Saved references to their old
locations in this folder must be updated to the archived paths. See
../pixel_transparency_experiments/README.txt for retained experiments and
the notes archive for retired routes.

Performance context
-------------------

The promoted Sunlight Shimmer program is the seven-pass Adaptive RPC profile
measured on Retroid Pocket Classic on 2026-10-15. At unchanged quality defaults,
uncapped scene medians were 62.53 FPS (Mario Tennis screen fit), 70.66 (integer),
72.21 (Pokemon Lazarus screen fit), and 76.38 (integer). Normal playback with
audio/VSync measured approximately 60 FPS. These are historical measurements
of that program, not a new benchmark of every LCD variant or a thermal soak.
The archived ADAPTIVE-RPC.txt records the method and its limitations.
