Sunlight Shimmer adaptive performance candidate
==============================================

sunlight_shimmer-adaptive-fast-authentic_gbc_fast.slangp

Use this as a separate comparison against the existing adaptive shimmer preset.
It keeps the same seven passes, LCD stages, parameter defaults, full paper
texture, seven rainbow colors, and 256x256 light field. Existing presets remain
unchanged. The source-size bypass is not restored.

The ordinary adaptive compositor includes a direct nine-sample shadow kernel
for positions outside the source image. This variant caches a moving window
of source cells, including those positions, before the final compositor runs.
The final compositor uses one shadow fetch and no direct kernel. This aims to
reduce the cost of combining transparency and sunlight in one large shader.

The allocation is 6W by 2H, covering three horizontal phases for a (W+1) by
(H+1) window even with very small inputs. Only the active window is shaded;
other texels are discarded. Cache memory is four times the ordinary adaptive
cache, so lower final-shader cost is a tradeoff, not a measured speed guarantee.
As with the other adaptive profiles, keep the compositor last and preserve
the configured nearest filtering and paired cache/compositor.

Local shader builds and RetroArch Vulkan comparisons cover GB, GBA, bordered,
upscaled and odd-sized inputs, both scaling modes, blur, offsets and bypass.
Native GB/GBA captures match the ordinary adaptive preset exactly in these
tests, including the tested GBA blur and offset changes. Integer kernel tap
positions avoid unstable, very narrow phase intervals at larger sizes, but
change rounding at some sampling boundaries. The tested 640x480 default
differs by up to twelve 8-bit channel levels, and a zero-offset test reaches
fifteen; the largest mean channel error is 0.028 levels. Keep the ordinary
adaptive preset for comparison on upscaled sources. The existing sunlight
field interpolation approximation also remains.

Retroid Pocket Classic measurements (2026-10-15)
-----------------------------------------------

Vulkan, Adreno A12, RetroArch 1.22.2 (16734fe). All quality settings retain their
defaults: full paper texture, seven rainbow colors and Smooth shadow blur.
Each result is the median of three run medians over frames 128-320 after loading
the same copied save state. Audio, vsync, frame skipping and runahead are off
for these uncapped whole-application throughput measurements.

Scene / viewport                      Adaptive       Fast       Gain
Mario Tennis, fit 1200x1080             54.23 FPS     57.07 FPS    5.2%
Mario Tennis, integer 1120x1008         61.50 FPS     64.24 FPS    4.4%
Pokemon Lazarus, fit 1240x827           64.27 FPS     65.87 FPS    2.5%
Pokemon Lazarus, integer 1200x800       68.86 FPS     69.95 FPS    1.6%

Mario Tennis uses Gambatte and copied slot 9 on its title screen. Pokemon
Lazarus uses mGBA and copied slot 0 in an outdoor town scene. The original
GB-only RPC shimmer profile reaches 61.12 FPS at Mario Tennis screen fit.

With audio and vsync enabled, the fast profile measures approximately 60 FPS
in both GBA modes and integer-scaled GBC; GBC screen fit remains near 57 FPS.
The gain is modest and does not meet 60 FPS at GBC screen fit. These are
scene-specific checks, not a long thermal soak or a guarantee for every game.
Physical tilt remains unverified. Tests use isolated configuration and saves;
the normal device settings and original save states are preserved.
