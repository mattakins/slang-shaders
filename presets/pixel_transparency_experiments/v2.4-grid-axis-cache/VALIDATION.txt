This measures overall frame throughput, not isolated GPU time. It is a short
repeatability check, not a thermal soak. No new GBA, integer-scale, plain-PT or
subpixels-enabled throughput claims are made for the cached route.

Images
------
26 paired RPC configurations compared to the previous integrated route:
  16 default pairs: plain/Shimmer x below/above x GB/GBA x fit/integer.
   8 Shimmer GB-fit pairs: below/above x all four display-subpixel layouts.
   2 Shimmer GB-fit pairs: below/above with white lines.

These render native save-state PNGs through the built-in imageviewer core;
they are device captures but are not live gameplay. Motion was disabled on
both sides (PT_ACCEL_ENABLE=0). Each candidate was captured twice and matched
its repeat exactly. Original full-screen captures are retained; both sides
use the same known viewport crop. No image rescaling or colour adjustment was
used for measurements.

Default below-grid cases: all eight within 1/255 per RGB channel.
Default plain PT above: all four within 1/255.
Default Shimmer above: GB fit max 7/255, GBA fit max 2/255, both integer max 1/255.
Enabled subpixels: below max 1/255; above max 7–8/255, mean at most 0.0205/255.
White lines: below max 2/255; above max 7/255, mean 0.0416/255 or less.

These are encoded RGB differences, not perceptual thresholds. The new caches
use FP32 axis overlaps and deterministic output-pixel centres rather than the
previous interpolated full-screen FP16 coverage image. Matching is close but
not pixel-exact. Paper and lighting retain full output resolution.
