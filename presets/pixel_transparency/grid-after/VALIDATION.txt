Static saved frames, not live gameplay or Retroid captures.

All five LCD families, both Pixel Transparency and Sunlight Shimmer:
- Ten grid-before captures were pixel-identical to the existing presets.
- Forty grid-after captures rendered at GB/GBA fit and integer scale.
- Ten effects-bypass captures rendered successfully.

The window was 1240x1080. GB fit: 1200x1080; GB integer: 1120x1008;
GBA fit: 1240x827; GBA integer: 1200x800. Five additional Shimmer captures
used stretched GBA at 1240x1080. PT_ACCEL_ENABLE=0 for repeatability;
other settings retained the existing preset defaults, including full paper,
seven rainbow colours and Smooth shadows. Logs contained no shader errors
or fallback. Runtime logs confirmed 16-bit float effects and LCD targets.

Additional checks:
- Repeated LCD1x and LCD Grid v2 Shimmer captures were pixel-identical.
- LCD Grid v2 and Simpletex rendered with PT_HIDE_CONTENT enabled.
- Plain transparency with final dither on versus off differed by at most
  1/255 per channel (615254 changed pixels in the LCD1x GB fit capture).
- Shimmer output was unchanged by PT_DEFER_DITHER, as intended: Shimmer
  performs its own final dither. The effects bypass added no dither.
- Shimmer rendered with sunlight disabled.

Twenty vertex/fragment stage builds passed glslangValidator and spirv-val,
covering sensor uniforms enabled and disabled for all five new shader entries.
All twenty presets resolved their shader/texture dependencies. The original
LCD files, twelve standard presets and existing shared shader files were
unchanged (37 checked source/preset hashes).

Grid-after intentionally changes presentation. The effects compositor is
intermediate in these fixed-order variants, so raster coordinates/rounding
can also change boundary sampling and paper-grain phase. These variants do
not claim pixel parity with the standard presets or the separate Authentic
integration, whose compositor remains final. LCD Grid v2 additionally avoids
native-centre downsampling by using output-resolution colour samples.

No RPC performance measurement or live-game timing was taken for these
variants. More framebuffer bandwidth is required than the standard route.
