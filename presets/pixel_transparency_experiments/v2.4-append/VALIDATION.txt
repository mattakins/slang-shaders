All-append candidate validation (2026-10-16)
==========================================

Mac RetroArch Vulkan; built-in imageviewer; native saved-frame images rendered
at frame 10, quality defaults and PT_ACCEL_ENABLE=0. Mario Tennis uses the
Gambatte slot 9 thumbnail (160x144); Pokemon Lazarus uses the mGBA saved-frame
thumbnail (240x160). These are static renders, not live gameplay or RPC tests.

- All 15 candidate presets load with both GB and GBA inputs: 30 captures.
- Controls, ordering, labels, defaults, ranges, explicit parameter values,
  textures and pass counts match their corresponding standard presets.
- Every one of the 42 original production files is restored byte-for-byte;
  thirteen external LCD/other shaders and textures also remain unchanged.
- All 95 related preset/reference chains and their include/texture paths resolve.
- Twenty-four candidate vertex/fragment builds validate with sensor uniforms
  enabled and disabled. Eight additional reference builds validate; all eight
  compositor stage binaries match the existing generic append implementation.
- Both families match the standard native-cache output exactly on Authentic
  GBC Fast, lcd1x, lcd3x and lcd-grid-v2 at GB/GBA screen fit: sixteen pairs.
- Authentic GBC Fast Shimmer also matches at GB/GBA integer scale. Its screen
  fit capture repeats exactly and both final relocated preset paths render
  the same images as their tested versions.
- The standalone presets reproduce the existing generic append route exactly
  for both inputs: four pairs. Their fixed cache and larger-input direct-kernel
  fallback retain the existing append implementation.
- Simpletex and zfast are not image-identical to the standard native-cache
  versions. Their tested peak channel differences range from 16 to 82/255.
- Frontlit also differs: peaks of 128/255 on GB and 76/255 on GBA. Glass remains
  last. Its feedback shader explicitly samples PassFeedback0; the append
  layout runs that shader as pass zero instead of pass two, changing which
  previous-frame output it receives. No LCD or feedback shader was edited.

These differences are why the candidate remains separate. Standard defaults
and the measured native-cache pipeline are preserved. RPC-BENCHMARKS.txt records
48 new Retroid timing runs, six normal-playback checks and four pixel-identical
GB/GBA image pairs. Physical tilt and live Append Preset menu interaction remain
unverified, as do other graphics backends. Launch/capture duration is not an
isolated GPU benchmark.
