Pixel Transparency v2.3 archive
==============================

This directory preserves the programs and presets present before the v2.4
Adaptive RPC promotion: Original, RPC, Adaptive, Adaptive Fast, Adaptive RPC,
Match, all LCD combinations, the standalone append entries, and the external
authentic-gbc-frontlit preset. Standalone entries are under standalone/.

Shader paths resolve to handheld/shaders/pixel_transparency_experiments/v2.3/.
LCD and other external shaders/textures retain their existing shared paths.
Only relative references were rebased; shader calculations, preset values,
parameter metadata, and file-ending styles are preserved.

The notes beside these presets retain their original measurements and known
limitations. MANIFEST.json maps the former locations to their archived files.
Historical named presets are available directly in this directory. Their
shortcuts in presets/pixel_transparency/ have been removed to keep the current
catalog clear. Update saved references to those old filenames to point here.

Earlier v1.1/v2.0/v2.1/v2.2 comparisons and other experiments stay in the parent
experimental catalog; they are not overwritten by this archive.
