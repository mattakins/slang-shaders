Pixel Transparency v2.4 validation (2026-10-16)
==============================================

Mac RetroArch Vulkan, native saved-frame images rendered with the built-in
imageviewer at frame 10, full quality defaults and motion disabled. Sources:
Mario Tennis/Gambatte slot 9 thumbnail (160x144) and Pokemon Lazarus/mGBA slot 0
thumbnail (240x160). These are static frame comparisons, not new RPC captures.

- All 80 remaining related preset/reference chains resolve across the repository.
- All 15 production presets preserve parameter IDs, ordering, labels, defaults
  and ranges except the explicit v2.4 version heading.
- Every production preset loads both native GB and GBA inputs: 30 captures.
- The Authentic GBC Fast Shimmer profile matches Adaptive RPC exactly at fit
  and integer scales on both inputs. Plain transparency matches the same RPC
  compositor with sunlight disabled and the plain profile's color settings.
  Eight promoted/reference image pairs are pixel-identical.
- Four appended standalone/paired image pairs match exactly on GB and GBA.
- All 22 archived presets reproduce their pre-promotion captures exactly at GB
  screen fit. Archived shader expansions retain their original calculations
  and metadata after path rebasing. The seven redundant historical shortcuts
  were removed from the production preset catalog; archived profiles remain.
- All 72 active/reference vertex and fragment compilations pass SPIR-V
  validation, with sensor uniforms enabled and disabled. The promoted Shimmer
  compositor and Authentic GBC Fast cache match the Adaptive RPC stage binaries
  exactly in all eight stage/sensor combinations.
- The standalone cache's 511x511 capacity boundary matches a larger-cache
  reference. At 512x512 and 640x480, both standalone compositors match a direct
  complete-kernel reference exactly. Six synthetic-input comparisons pass.
- Thirteen shared LCD/other source or texture dependencies remain unchanged.
- A repeated production Shimmer capture is identical.
- Generic append chains load with Authentic GBC Fast, lcd1x, lcd3x, lcd-grid-v2,
  simpletex_lcd, zfast-lcd and the four-pass lcd-shader preset. Plain transparency
  uses the GB input and Shimmer the GBA input. The checks retain each grid's
  original pass order, settings, aliases and textures: 14 saved-frame captures.
- Four additional grid-only/append-disabled pairs preserve the grid image
  exactly. A paired Authentic GBC Fast Shimmer capture with
  the generic cache matches its LCD-specific wrapper exactly. These append
  checks total 23 Mac Vulkan captures; they do not benchmark RPC performance
  or verify the interactive Append Preset menu itself.

No new Retroid throughput or physical-tilt measurements were taken for this
promotion. Prior Adaptive RPC measurements apply to that unchanged rendering
program; the append-compatible route and other LCD combinations have not been
benchmarked on the Retroid. Native-cache allocations still scale with input
size and remain subject to the device's texture and memory limits.
