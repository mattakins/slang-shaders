Integrated Authentic grid: Retroid Pocket Classic — 2026-10-17
=============================================================

Conclusion
----------
The separate-pass integrated grid preserves the default image, but does not
meet the general 60 FPS RPC target. Keep the current fast paired presets as
the performance recommendation. This experiment has not been promoted.

Method
------
- Actual RPC, Vulkan, reported GPU "Adreno (TM) A12".
- RetroArch 1.22.2, build 16734fe, com.retroarch.aarch64.
- Mario Tennis/Gambatte: copied slot 9 auto-loaded at each launch.
- Pokemon Lazarus/mGBA: copied slot 0 auto-loaded at each launch.
- Sample the same frames 128-320 after loading, before the title scene changes.
- Read the visible FPS/frame counter from device screenshots; retain each
  screenshot, OCR result, frame number, log, config and preset snapshot.
- Uncapped runs disable audio, VSync, frameskip and runahead. No fast-forward.
- GB fit: 1200x1080; integer: 1120x1008. GBA fit: 1240x827;
  integer: 1200x800. Device render surface: 1240x1080.
- 45 uncapped runs plus 3 normal-playback checks. One initial reader warmup
  attempt missed the sampling window and was discarded.
- Battery readings during the reported runs were 23-24 C. These are not GPU
  temperatures; the Android thermal service exposed no temperature readings.

These are short matched-scene throughput measurements, not isolated GPU pass
timings or a thermal soak. Plain transparency timings cover GB fit only.
No performance claim is made for white lines or enabled subpixel rendering.
The earlier reported 22 FPS belongs to the fused experiment, not this build.
The new grid stages and framebuffer traffic need profiling before attributing
the slowdown to any single pass or promising an optimization gain.

Image comparisons and subpixel controls
--------------------------------------
38 unique on-device configurations, each checked with two identical raw
captures. Static native save-state PNGs rendered by RetroArch imageviewer;
these image comparisons are separate from the live-game benchmarks above.
PT_ACCEL_ENABLE=0 for deterministic lighting. Preset defaults otherwise kept,
except the explicitly selected grid placement, white lines or subpixel layout.

- All eight default grid-below pairs are pixel-identical to the current fast
  preset: both effects, both games, fit and integer scaling.
- Authentic's RGB cell geometry is retained in both placements.
- Enable subpixel rendering and all four layouts (RGB, RGB vertical, BGR,
  BGR vertical) render successfully in both placements. The four Shimmer GB
  fit grid-below captures also match the current preset pixel-for-pixel.
- Grid-above intentionally places the RGB pattern over paper and lighting;
  it looks different and is not expected to match grid-below.
- Both placements render with white lines. These retain Authentic's geometry,
  not Simpletex's line shape. Fine paper/lighting remain at output resolution.
- Final dither remains enabled. Paired screenshots use identical measured
  viewport crops; no resampling, recolouring or brightness normalization.
